title: "Government Transparency and Corporate Innovation Disclosure"
collection: publications
authors: "Job market paper"
abstract: "This study examines whether government transparency influences firms’ voluntary innovation disclosure. Using the staggered adoption of state-level open data websites in the United States as a plausibly exogenous shock, I implement a difference-in-differences design with a sample of U.S. public firms from 2008 to 2020. I find that firms headquartered in states that launch open data websites subsequently increase their voluntary innovation disclosure. The effect is stronger for firms facing greater product market and talent competition, and for those operating in more corrupt environments. Additional analyses show that improvements in government transparency amplify peer disclosure spillovers and enhance firms’ innovation outputs. Overall, the findings highlight the role of public-sector transparency in shaping corporate information environments and innovation."
